Half-life

Every decision at its real moment, decaying while it stands unconfirmed

landed 0/0 · chattering 0 · t = +0s · playback 20x

Rendered from spans this build already emits - captured by docs/visuals/capture_run.py straight out of the OpenTelemetry SDK, no new instrumentation. A decision's moment is the epoch OpenRouter embeds in gen_ai.response.id; it counts as confirmed when a toy-world-outcomes span with augmentloop.grade.source = reality shares that id. Reality verdicts carry no timestamp of their own in this run, so nothing on this page places a verdict in time. What the verdicts said when they landed is the regret ledger's story (regret-ledger.html). One recording, one window - the sample is what it is.
